# Stage 8 test

This build only logs more about the horse, so the controls should feel the same as in stage 7. The logs need clean stretches at each speed, so hold each gait steady for the full time and don't steer unless a step says to.

## Setup

1. Close the game. In DMM, turn `HorseSteer` off and on again and apply.
2. Load your save and wait until at least 20 seconds after launch.
3. Mount your horse on open, flat ground if you can.

## Steps

1. **Stand still for 5 seconds.**
2. **Walk for 10 seconds, then release W and wait 5 seconds.**
3. **Trot for 10 seconds, then release W and wait 5 seconds.**
4. **Gallop in a straight line for 60 seconds, then release W and wait until the horse stops, plus 5 seconds.** The long run is to cross map chunk boundaries.
5. **From standing, hold S for 10 seconds, then release and wait 5 seconds.**
6. **Walk, then steer with A and D in turn for 10 seconds.**
7. **Taming minigame, only if you happen to get one:** if the horse freezes after a win, stay frozen for 10 seconds and note the time.

Close the game and leave the logs in place until they've been read.
