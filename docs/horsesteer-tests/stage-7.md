# Stage 7 test

The game was patched, so this build of the mod has new addresses and no other changes. Everything should behave as it did in stage 6.

## Setup

1. Close the game. In DMM, turn `HorseSteer` off and on again and apply.
2. Load your save and wait until at least 20 seconds after launch.

## Steps

1. **Mount your horse and hold W at a walk, trot and gallop, moving the camera around:** the horse should keep its heading, and A or D should steer it.
2. **From standing, hold S with A or D:** the horse should pivot.
3. **From standing, hold S for about 5 seconds, then release:** the horse should walk backward, then return to idle.
4. **At a gallop, release W and hold S at once:** the horse should stop, then walk backward.
5. **Dismount, then mount again:** the controls should work as before.

Close the game and leave the logs in place until they've been read.
